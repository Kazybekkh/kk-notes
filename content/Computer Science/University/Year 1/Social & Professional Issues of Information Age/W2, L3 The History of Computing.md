# Babbage

- ## The Analytical Engine
	- **1837** Babbage describes a general-purpose programmable computing machine: the analytical engine
		1. input
		2. Store = Memory
		3. Mill = Central Processing Unit (CPU)
		4. Output
- ## The Difference Engine No. 2
	- **1847 - 1849** Babbage develops the Difference Engine No. 2, minimised version
- A life of personal tragedies
	- Wife, Kids' death
- Passes away in 1871
