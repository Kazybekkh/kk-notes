# Your robot learning book

Your direction: build a strong research foundation, pursue a US master's with meaningful research opportunities, contribute to a robot-learning company such as Physical Intelligence, and eventually build a useful robotics business. The bridge between these goals is evidence: mathematical understanding, reliable systems, thoughtful experiments, and clear communication.

This is a living textbook and lab notebook. It covers the central ideas and opens paths into deeper topics; no finite collection can cover the entire field. Your current starting assets are a CS background, existing AI course notes, an SO-101 workflow, Isaac Sim, and a collection of robot-learning papers.

> [!tip] Your next session
> Read [[01 Math for Learning]], solve its exercise before opening the answer, then create your first experiment using [[Experiment Template]]. Keep one small physical task in mind: moving a block between two marked positions.
