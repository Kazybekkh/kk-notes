# A 24-week learning path

[[Home]] | [[Mastery Tracker]] | [[18 Masters Research and Startup Path]]

This is a suggested sequence, not an assumption about your available time. Plan around 8-12 focused hours per week initially; extend a phase when its mastery checks are not yet met. Coursework and exams take priority over an artificial deadline.

| Weeks | Study | Build | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Math and probability, chapters 1-2 | [[Lab 01 Planar Arm]] | Derivations, Jacobian checks, geometry plots |
| 3-4 | Dynamics, perception, chapters 3-4 | Feedback and scalar estimation examples | Explain frames, units, delay, uncertainty |
| 5-6 | Neural policies and imitation, chapters 5-6 | Small dataset and training sanity checks | Split manifest, loss plots, failure examples |
| 7-8 | Action chunks, chapter 7 | [[Lab 02 Policy Baseline]] | Repeatable robot task and baseline report |
| 9-10 | Generative policies, chapter 8 | Compare an existing second policy if feasible | Matched-data comparison and latency |
| 11-12 | RL foundations and offline RL, chapters 9-10 | Small simulated benchmark using existing library | Bellman derivation and multi-seed report |
| 13-14 | World models and simulation, chapter 11 | One controlled simulator-to-reality comparison | Documented model mismatch |
| 15-16 | Human data, VLA, contact, chapters 12-14 | Upgrade five paper notes using full texts | Evidence table and related-work map |
| 17-18 | Deployment and evaluation, chapters 15-16 | [[Lab 03 Controlled Generalization]] | Held-out test protocol and intervals |
| 19-20 | Research methods, chapter 17 | Pilot one question from [[Research Dashboard]] | Preregistered hypothesis and ablation |
| 21-22 | Uncertainty or chosen specialization | [[Lab 04 Calibration]] or expanded project | Results that survive a second session |
| 23-24 | Writing and career reflection, chapter 18 | Technical report, demo, reproducible repository | Supervisor feedback and next-study plan |

## A repeatable week

- Two study sessions: derive an equation and answer a chapter exercise without notes.
- Two build sessions: implement or reproduce a small result.
- One reading session: annotate one full paper with [[Reading Template]].
- One review: record failures, changed beliefs, and the next smallest useful test.

If you have four hours rather than ten, keep all four activity types but reduce their size. Reading twenty papers without building one baseline can leave a false sense of progress.

## Three checkpoints

After week 8, you should be able to explain every stage from sensor capture to motor command. After week 16, you should compare methods by assumptions and data requirements, not just headline success. After week 24, aim for one defensible research report. These are targets to assess, not accomplishments already earned.
