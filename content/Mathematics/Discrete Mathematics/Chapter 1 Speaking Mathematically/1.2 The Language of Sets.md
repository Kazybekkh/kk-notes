# Subsets

![[Screenshot 2024-08-18 at 9.43.05 PM.png]]\
It follows from the definition of subset that for a set A not to be a subset of a set B means that there is at least one element of A that is not an element of B. Symbolically:
![[Screenshot 2024-08-18 at 9.45.39 PM.png]]
- Definition
Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. $A$ is a proper subset of $B$ if, and only if, every element of $A$ is in $B$ but there is at least one element of $B$ that is not in $A$.
